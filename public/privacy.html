Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

EduPing ("EduPing", "we", "us") is a WhatsApp-based communication and management platform for schools, provided by Sx Creations Company Ltd, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission, Nigeria. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to schools, parents, teachers, and administrators who use EduPing.

1. Who this policy applies to

EduPing is provided to schools ("Client Schools") under an agreement with each school. Parents, teachers, and administrators interact with EduPing through WhatsApp on behalf of, and at the direction of, the Client School. The Client School remains the data controller for student and parent information; EduPing acts as a data processor providing the platform.

2. Information we collect

  • From schools: staff lists, class structures, subjects, fee structures, and school contact details, provided during onboarding.
  • From parents and guardians: name, phone number, and messages sent to the school's WhatsApp number.
  • From teachers and staff: name, phone number, photo-verified sign-in images, attendance records, homework and grade submissions sent via WhatsApp.
  • About students: name, class, attendance, academic performance, and fee status, as provided by the Client School and its staff.
  • Automatically: message timestamps and delivery metadata needed to operate the WhatsApp integration (via Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and/or Twilio).

3. How we use this information

  • To route and respond to WhatsApp messages between parents, teachers, and the school's AI assistant.
  • To generate attendance records, homework alerts, performance summaries, and fee status updates.
  • To power the school's admin dashboard and reporting features.
  • To improve the reliability and accuracy of EduPing's AI responses.

We do not sell student, parent, or staff data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Third-party processors

To deliver WhatsApp messaging, EduPing uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and/or Twilio as messaging infrastructure providers. AI-generated responses are produced using a third-party AI model provider. These providers process message content solely to deliver and generate messages on EduPing's behalf and are bound by their own data processing terms.

5. Data storage and security

Data is stored on secured servers with access restricted to authorized EduPing personnel and the relevant Client School's administrators. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Data retention

We retain school, parent, and student data for as long as the Client School's account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, academic record-keeping, or dispute-resolution needs, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

7. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act and applicable regulations, parents, teachers, and staff may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data by contacting their school directly, or by reaching EduPing using the details below. Requests concerning a specific student's data should be directed to the Client School, as the data controller.

8. Children's data

EduPing processes student data only as instructed by and on behalf of the Client School, which is responsible for obtaining any necessary parental consent under applicable law before enrolling a student's information into the platform.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
