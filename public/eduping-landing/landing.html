Excel spreadsheets, missed calls, and overflowing WhatsApp inboxes

Out of the loop

Schools cannot identify at-risk students early or generate meaningful reports without hours of manual work.

The Founder

Built by someone who
understands schools, not just software

Before EduPing, founder Philip Igwebuike spent years consulting for Nigerian private schools on brand positioning and enrollment strategy — trained in visual and brand communication at Keyline Digital Communications, Durban.

One pattern kept surfacing: schools that communicated well with parents kept them — and earned referrals from them. Schools that didn't, lost both. Most schools were already trying to solve this the only way they knew how: dedicated WhatsApp groups, with all the chaos that comes with them.

Nearly every school management platform on the market was built to improve admin efficiency — almost none were built for parents, on the channel parents actually use every day. EduPing was built to close that gap: instead of asking parents to adopt a new app, we built where they already live.

Where this is going

Parent communication is the entry point, not the ceiling. As EduPing learns more about each child through everyday school-parent interaction, that same infrastructure becomes the foundation for personalized learning — turning routine communication into a data layer schools can act on, at a scale no single school could build alone.
